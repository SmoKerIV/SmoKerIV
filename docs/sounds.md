# CC0 sound sources for the tavern

Research date: 2026-10-07. Every URL below was loaded during research. Licences are as stated on each item's own page
(Freesound: "Creative Commons 0"; OpenGameArt: "CC0" listed alone unless noted; Kenney: "Creative Commons CC0").
Nothing was downloaded. Durations and formats come from the Freesound page header; "quality" notes come from the
description, download counts and ratings only. Nothing was listened to, so audition before committing.

Caveats:
- Loopability is NOT verifiable from pages. For ambience, plan a crossfade loop (equal-power, 2-4 s) in ffmpeg/Audacity.
- Kenney pack file names were not shown on the pack pages, so slot-to-file mapping inside Kenney zips is unverified.
- Freesound needs a free login to download. Kenney and OpenGameArt zips are direct.
- Freesound CC0 means no credit needed, but keep a `CREDITS.md` of ids anyway for provenance.

Common processing: mono 48 kHz (loops: fire mono, room tone and wind stereo), high-pass for DC and rumble (25-350 Hz depending on the sound), 3-30 ms fade-in and 30 ms-2.5 s fade-out, encoded to Opus/WebM (40 kbps one-shots, 48-64 kbps loops) and AAC/M4A (1.5x the bitrate).
One-shot level: gain = min(category RMS target - clip RMS, -3 dBFS - peak); targets are spells -19, sfx -22, ambience accents -27, ui -34 dBFS RMS.
Loops: equal-power (qsin) crossfade of the tail into the head, then integrated-loudness gain and a -3 dBFS limiter.
